Physical Mouth Mechanics

How does the circonflexe actually change the shape of your mouth? Toggle the sounds below to see the mechanics in action!

ê as in "crêpe"

Open Sound [ɛ]

Mouth Action Plan:

  • Drop your jaw downward slightly.
  • Keep your tongue resting low.
  • Feels relaxed, like saying the English word get.

ô as in "hôtel"

Closed Sound [o]

Mouth Action Plan:

  • Raise your jaw to narrow the gap.
  • Round your lips into a tight circle.
  • Feels tense, like saying the English word no (without gliding to "w").

â as in "pâte"

Deep Sound [ɑ]

Mouth Action Plan:

  • Drop your jaw wide open.
  • Pull your tongue low and toward the back.
  • Darker and deeper than regular "a" — like the doctor's aah.

î as in "île"

Smile Sound [i]

Mouth Action Plan:

  • Spread your lips into a tight smile.
  • Push your tongue high and forward.
  • Sounds exactly like plain "i" — like the vowel in see, but short and crisp.

û as in "sûr"

Whistle Sound [y]

Mouth Action Plan:

  • Say "ee" as in see… then round your lips like a whistle without moving your tongue.
  • Tongue stays high and forward; only the lips change.
  • No English equivalent — it is NOT "oo"!
