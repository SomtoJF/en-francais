Le passé récent 🕒

Say what has just happened with venir de + an infinitive.

An action that just happened

The passé récent describes an action that happened very recently. Use the present tense of venir + de + an infinitive: Je viens de manger. means “I have just eaten.”

Venir: je viens, tu viens, il/elle/on vient, nous venons, vous venez, ils/elles viennent.

Use d’ before a vowel sound: Elle vient d’arriver. She has just arrived.

Keep the action verb infinitive: Nous venons de finir. We have just finished.

Earlier past viewpoint: imperfect venir de + infinitive can mean “had just”: Il venait de partir quand j’ai appelé. He had just left when I called.
