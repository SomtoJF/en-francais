Le conditionnel présent 💭

What would happen, what we politely ask for, and what we imagine.

A hypothetical or polite action

The conditionnel présent describes what would happen, often hypothetical, polite, or uncertain. Je parlerais français. → “I would speak French.”

Build it: futur simple stem + imparfait endings.

Endings: -ais, -ais, -ait, -ions, -iez, -aient.

Si rule: si + imparfait → conditionnel; not si + conditionnel in this beginner pattern.

Use it for polite requests, hypothetical results, uncertainty, and negation.
