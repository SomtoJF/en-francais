L'Accent Aigu ↗ é

The rising accent that turns "e" into a crisp "ay" — and the only accent that lives on just one letter.

One Letter Only: é

The accent aigu exists only on the letter e — you will never see á, í, ó, or ú in French. It rises to the right (↗) and produces the closed sound [e], like "ay" in "day" but without the y-glide: été, café, école, vélo.

Memory trick for the direction:

Aigu rises ↗ like the pitch of your voice on "ay!" — grave falls ↘ like the deeper, open "air" sound. If the word sounds like "ay", it climbs: é.

é or è? Which Way Does the Hat Tilt?

The classic mix-up: aigu (é ↗) vs grave (è ↘). Listen to the word, feel the sound, and pick the right accent.
