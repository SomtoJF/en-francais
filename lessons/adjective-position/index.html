Where Do French Adjectives Go? 📍

Descriptive adjectives usually follow the noun. A small group, remembered with BAGS, often goes before it.

Descriptive adjectives add a quality

They describe a noun. In French, most come after the noun: une voiture rouge means “a red car.” Some common short adjectives go before it.

Usually after

une robe bleue = a blue dress
un film intéressant = an interesting film

BAGS often goes before

Beauty: joli. Age: jeune. Goodness: bon. Size: petit.
une jolie petite maison = a pretty little house

Two adjective jobs: descriptive adjectives add a quality, as in une robe bleue (a blue dress). Limiting adjectives identify or count the noun, often in the article's place, as in cette robe (this dress). Study the seven limiting types →

BAGS is a memory aid, not an unbreakable rule. Adjective position can change meaning: un ancien professeur is a former teacher, while un meuble ancien is an antique piece of furniture.
