Le futur simple 🔮

What will happen, with clear endings and useful irregular stems.

An action that will happen

The futur simple is a tense for future actions. Je parlerai demain. → “I will speak tomorrow.”

Endings: -ai, -as, -a, -ons, -ez, -ont.

Stem: infinitive; -re verbs drop final e.

Irregular stems: aur-, ser-, ir-, fer-, voudr-, pourr-, devr-, viendr-, verr-, saur-.

Si rule: si + présent → futur, never future directly after si.
