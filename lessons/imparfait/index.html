L’imparfait 🌧️

The tense of habits, background, and actions in progress.

An ongoing or repeated past action

L’imparfait is a past tense for ongoing, repeated, habitual, or background actions. Je parlais quand il est arrivé. → “I was speaking when he arrived.”

Stem: present nous form minus -ons.

Endings: -ais, -ais, -ait, -ions, -iez, -aient.

Exception: être → ét-.

Use it for habits, descriptions, age, weather, time, and background; use passé composé for completed events.
