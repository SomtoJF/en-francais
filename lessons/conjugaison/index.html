Conjugaison — ER · IR · RE 🔧

Present-tense conjugation of the three verb families — plus the irregular IR verbs (dormir, partir, venir…) that break the mold.

The Recipe: Stem + Ending

Chop the infinitive ending (-er, -ir, -re) off to get the stem, then add the ending that matches the subject. Regular IR verbs sneak an extra -iss- into the plural.

-ER · parler (to speak)

jeparle
tuparles
il/elleparle
nousparlons
vousparlez
ils/ellesparlent

-IR · finir (to finish)

jefinis
tufinis
il/ellefinit
nousfinissons
vousfinissez
ils/ellesfinissent

-RE · vendre (to sell)

jevends
tuvends
il/ellevend (nothing!)
nousvendons
vousvendez
ils/ellesvendent

The Rebels: Irregular IR Verbs

Not every -ir verb follows the finir pattern (no -iss- here). Three gangs to know:

The dormir gang

dormir, partir, sortir, servir, sentir, mentir — the singular drops the stem's last consonant, the plural keeps it:

je dors, tu dors, il dort
nous dormons, vous dormez, ils dorment

The venir gang

venir, tenir (+ devenir, revenir…) — the stem morphs to -ien- everywhere except nous/vous:

je viens, tu viens, il vient
nous venons, vous venez, ils viennent

The ouvrir gang

ouvrir, offrir, couvrir, souffrir — IR costume, ER heart: they take regular -ER endings:

j'ouvre, tu ouvres, il ouvre
nous ouvrons, vous ouvrez, ils ouvrent

Pick the Form

Regular ER, IR and RE verbs mixed together. Match the ending to the subject — watch out for the sneaky -iss- on plural IR forms.
