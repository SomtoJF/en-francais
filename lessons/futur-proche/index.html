Le futur proche 🚀

Talk about what is going to happen with aller + an infinitive.

A planned or near-future action

The futur proche describes something that is going to happen. Build it with the present tense of aller followed by an infinitive: Je vais parler. means “I am going to speak.”

Aller: je vais, tu vas, il/elle/on va, nous allons, vous allez, ils/elles vont.

Keep the second verb infinitive: Nous allons choisir. We are going to choose.

Negation wraps aller: Elle ne va pas sortir. She is not going to go out.

Pronouns go before the infinitive: Je vais le faire. I am going to do it.
