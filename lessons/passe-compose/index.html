Le passé composé ⏱️

Completed actions in the past, built one helper and one participle at a time.

A completed past action

The passé composé is a past tense for a completed action. J’ai parlé. → “I spoke / I have spoken.”

Build it: subject + avoir/être + past participle.

Remember: with être, the participle agrees: Elle est arrivée.

Negation: Je n’ai pas parlé.

Advanced: avoir normally has no agreement; a preceding COD can trigger it.
